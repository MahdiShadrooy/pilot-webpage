To discourage the inappropriate use of AI tools, assignments will not be evaluated in the traditional manner. Submitting written solutions to the system will not carry any direct credit. Instead, grades will be determined primarily through quizzes.

That said, students who attend the problem-solving session and independently solve two of the assigned questions may receive up to 50% of the corresponding quiz score.

As emphasized earlier, the primary objective of this policy is to encourage meaningful engagement with the course concepts rather than superficial completion of assignments.

The practical component of the course will follow the same structure as in previous years.

Each quiz will be administered one week after the assignment is released. Once the quiz is completed, the official solutions will be published, followed by the problem-solving session.
